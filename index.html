Add to an iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari on the iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Open the atlas from the home screen: it runs full screen, with no browser bars.
  4. Inside it, open the panels (☰) and press “Save for offline use” to store the whole atlas on the device.

After that it works with no connection, and a lecture hall's wifi stops mattering.
